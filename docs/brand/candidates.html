Engawa mark candidates

Eight candidates for the mark, for the user to choose from. Each is one path on a 64 unit grid in one colour (the current text colour), with no text and no gradient. Every row shows it at 16, 32, 64 and 180 px in the accent rose #ad235d, on the paper colour and on white, in the top bar (as it is live: the mark and Engawa), as the home page heading (縁側 Engawa), in a browser tab, and as the app icon (white on a rounded rose tile, at 180 px and at the 32 px favicon size). The previous mark, the roofed speech bubble, is the last row, for comparison. The sources are in docs/brand/candidates/01.svg to 08.svg. 04 was chosen on 4 November and replaced the bubble on the site; its two variants are compared in mark-04-variants.html.

01 Sitting on the edge 縁に腰掛ける

A neighbour sits on the veranda's edge, legs over the side; the two lines are the floor and the ground.

The most literal engawa and the warmest; at 16 px it can pass for a person at a desk or a priority seat.
